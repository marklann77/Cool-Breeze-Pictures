About

Cool Breeze Pictures was founded in 2026 by Mark Lannaman, bringing together his past work and a place for future work. Mark spent close to four years as an environmental reporter, and has produced stories for NPR and Saporta Report. That reporting background informs how he approaches documentary filmmaking. Mark, born and raised in Atlanta, has family roots in Jamaica and Colombia — all of which show up in the stories he chooses to tell. Aside from Cool Breeze, he works to advance sustainable practices in the food-water-energy nexus, earning three masters degrees from Georgia Tech by 23 and is still active in sustainable ventures today.

Additionally, Mark has worked on a number of screenplays, exploring themes of gratitude, responsibility, and the human condition. Reach out to read one of the password-protected screenplays!
